# VENUE_003: Ironworks Loft

## Overview
Ironworks Loft is a converted industrial warehouse at 88 Foundry Street,
favored for product launches, tech conferences, and corporate parties
wanting an exposed-brick, high-ceiling aesthetic.

## Capacity
The main loft floor holds 500 guests standing or 280 seated theater-style.
A mezzanine level adds seating for 80 more guests and is included in the
base rental.

## Pricing
Base rental is $9,500 for an 8-hour block, with a $750/hour overage fee.
A 15% deposit is due at signing, with the balance due 14 days before the
event.

## Cancellation Policy
Cancellations more than 60 days before the event forfeit only the deposit.
Cancellations within 60 days forfeit the deposit plus 25% of the remaining
balance. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the full contracted amount.
Corporate clients with a signed master services agreement are eligible for
a one-time date-transfer waiver in lieu of cancellation fees.

## Catering
No in-house catering; clients must use one of eight licensed caterers from
the building's approved list, each independently priced. A $400 kitchen-use
fee applies to any caterer using the on-site prep kitchen.

## AV and Equipment
Full production package (line-array sound, moving-head lighting, 4K
projection) is available as an add-on for $3,200/day. Basic house sound and
lighting are included in the base rental.

## International Guest Considerations
Ironworks Loft does not provide visa support letters or multilingual
signage; clients hosting international attendees should arrange this
independently.
