# POLICY_001: Cross-Venue Booking and Travel Policy

## Overview
This document covers policies that apply across all venues managed by the
Meridian Events Group (Grand Meridian Conference Center, Willowbrook Garden
Pavilion, and Ironworks Loft), including rules specific to international
bookings.

## International Bookings
For events with attendees traveling internationally, the events office
requires a finalized guest list 30 days in advance to process visa
invitation letters. International corporate bookings additionally require a
signed currency-hedging addendum if the contract is paid in a non-USD
currency, since venue pricing is quoted in USD.

## Force Majeure
Any venue booking may be postponed without cancellation penalty if a
force-majeure event (natural disaster, government travel restriction,
declared public health emergency) prevents the event from occurring. This
overrides the standard per-venue cancellation policy.

## Multi-Event Discounts
Clients booking more than one venue within the same calendar year receive a
5% discount on the second and subsequent bookings, applied to the base
rental fee only (not catering or AV add-ons).

## Payment Terms
All deposits are non-transferable between venues. Final balances are due 14
days before the event date across all three venues, regardless of each
venue's individual cancellation window.
