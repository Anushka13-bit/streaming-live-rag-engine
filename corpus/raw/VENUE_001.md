# VENUE_001: Grand Meridian Conference Center

## Overview
The Grand Meridian Conference Center is a downtown venue located at 400 Harbor
Plaza. It offers five configurable event halls and is the flagship venue for
corporate conferences, product launches, and large weddings.

## Capacity
The Grand Ballroom holds up to 800 guests in theater-style seating or 450
guests banquet-style. The three breakout rooms each hold 60 guests
classroom-style. Maximum building occupancy including staff is 950.

## Pricing
Full-day rental of the Grand Ballroom is $12,000 on weekdays and $16,500 on
weekends. Breakout rooms rent separately at $1,200/day each. A 20% deposit is
required to hold a date.

## Cancellation Policy
Cancellations made more than 90 days before the event receive a full refund
minus a $500 administrative fee. Cancellations between 30 and 90 days receive
a 50% refund. Cancellations within 30 days of the event are non-refundable.
Date changes (not cancellations) are permitted once at no charge if requested
more than 45 days in advance.

## Catering
In-house catering is mandatory for events over 200 guests. The venue offers
three tiers: Classic ($85/person), Premium ($140/person), and Executive
($210/person), all including a plated dinner and non-alcoholic beverages.
Outside catering is permitted only for events under 200 guests, subject to a
$1,500 outside-caterer fee.

## AV and Equipment
Built-in AV includes a 20-foot LED screen, wireless microphones, and a house
sound system, included in the ballroom rental. Additional stage lighting
rigs rent for $600/day.

## International Guest Considerations
For events involving international attendees, the venue can arrange
multilingual signage and provides a currency-neutral payment desk. Visa
invitation letters for international guests can be requested through the
events office with 15 business days' notice.
