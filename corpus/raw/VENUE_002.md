# VENUE_002: Willowbrook Garden Pavilion

## Overview
Willowbrook Garden Pavilion is an outdoor-covered venue set in a botanical
garden, popular for weddings and smaller receptions. Located at 22 Willow
Lane, it has one primary pavilion and an adjoining lawn.

## Capacity
The pavilion seats 220 guests banquet-style or 300 standing. The adjoining
lawn can accommodate an additional 150 guests for cocktail-style overflow.
There is no indoor backup space, so a tent add-on is recommended for
rain-risk dates.

## Pricing
Pavilion rental is $6,800 for a 10-hour block. The rain-contingency tent
add-on is $2,200. A 25% deposit secures the booking.

## Cancellation Policy
Cancellations more than 120 days out receive a full refund minus a $300
processing fee. Cancellations between 60 and 120 days receive a 40% refund.
No refunds are issued inside 60 days. The rain-contingency tent fee is
non-refundable once the tent is reserved, regardless of notice period.

## Catering
Willowbrook maintains a preferred-vendor list of five outside caterers; any
vendor from that list may be used at no additional fee. Caterers not on the
list require a $900 approval fee and a food-safety certificate on file two
weeks before the event.

## AV and Equipment
Outdoor-rated sound system and two wireless lapel mics are included.
Additional uplighting for the garden path is available at $450/event.

## Accessibility
Paved pathways connect parking to the pavilion; two accessible restrooms are
available on-site.
